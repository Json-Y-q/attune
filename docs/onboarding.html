Fit AI answers to how you think

Three short tasks (about 4 minutes) build a personal profile: how much, how fast and in what shape you like AI answers. Then the demo shows the same answer adapted to you.

Privacy: results stay in this browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to any server. You can export or delete them any time.

This is a comfort-preference tool, not an intelligence or health test. Results are rough and can vary day to day.

Number span

What it measures How many items you can hold in mind at once.
Where it is used Sets how much text Attune shows at a time.

Digits appear one at a time. Afterwards, type them in the same order and press Enter. The sequence gets longer while you are correct. This task is visual.

Reaction and focus

What it measures How steady your attention and reaction speed are.
Where it is used Sets how fast text arrives and when to suggest a break.

Wait for the box to turn green and say NOW, then press Space or Enter, or tap the box, as fast as you can. 20 rounds. Pressing early restarts the round.

Reading comfort

What it measures Your own preferences. There are no right answers.
Where it is used Sets the default amount, shape and pace of your answers.

Same text, different shapes. Pick what feels easiest to read without losing the point.
