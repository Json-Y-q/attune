Voice pace and conversation partner

A simulation of how a conversation partner could adapt when a chat gets heavy: speech speed, pauses and shorter replies, with quiet changes first. Text-to-speech runs on your device. The microphone is never used and there is no AI model.

Attune is not a medical device and does not diagnose or measure fatigue or health. It adjusts how the conversation is delivered (length, pace) for reading and listening comfort. All values here are simulated.

Starting point

Taken from your onboarding profile and today’s virtual condition.

Only lowers thresholds by one step. It never triggers anything on its own.

Mode

    Voice speed (text-to-speech)

    Reads the latest reply with a voice built into your device. Nothing is recorded.

    Below level 1 speed the voice is never slowed further; use longer pauses instead.

    Conversation simulator

    A scripted partner. Press the buttons to play what a user might do. Only counts, timings and the resume card are kept, never the conversation text.

    Signal panel

      Why did it change?

      Same meter and colours as the demo. Virtual, not a measurement.

      Conversation density

      Signals are simple rules on counts and timing. The thresholds are assumptions, not validated.

      Load feedback button

      Press when a chat feels too heavy. Attune immediately shortens and slows the simulated reply, and records a local label (timestamp, session id, recent turn count, signal snapshot). Labels stay in this browser only.

      Nothing is sent anywhere. Export downloads a JSON file to your device. Clear deletes the local log.

        Prototype label schema for training a feedback loop later. Not a medical measurement.

        Loop escape

        Notices when a chat goes round in circles (the same question again, the AI repeating itself, long with no progress, changing direction) and offers ways out. It uses simple rules inside this tab.

        • Implemented: rule-based local detection (word overlap, wording patterns in English and Korean, repeated advice and errors).
        • Planned, not built: LLM-based meaning check, browser extension, real chat integration.

        Nothing you paste is stored or sent anywhere. It is checked in this page only and disappears when you reload or press Clear.
