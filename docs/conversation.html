Voice pace and conversation partner

A simulation of how a conversation partner could adapt when a chat gets heavy: speech speed, pauses and shorter replies, with quiet changes first. Text-to-speech runs on your device. The microphone is never used and there is no AI model.

Attune is not a medical device and does not diagnose or measure fatigue or health. It adjusts how the conversation is delivered (length, pace) for reading and listening comfort. All values here are simulated.

Starting point

Taken from your onboarding profile and today’s virtual condition.

Only lowers thresholds by one step. It never triggers anything on its own.

Mode

    Voice speed (text-to-speech)

    Reads the latest reply with a voice built into your device. Nothing is recorded.

    Below level 1 speed the voice is never slowed further; use longer pauses instead.

    Conversation simulator

    A scripted partner. Press the buttons to play what a user might do. Only counts, timings and the resume card are kept, never the conversation text.

      Virtual silence timeline

      Text mode: waiting alone changes nothing (rule R5). Switch to Voice to see the timeline.

      1. 0–4 s: wait quietly
      2. 4–8 s: show a listening state (visual only)
      3. 8 s: one gentle line
      4. 15 s: offer repeat, summary or pause
      5. 25 s: pause and show where it stopped
      Stopped at: explaining sleep and memory
