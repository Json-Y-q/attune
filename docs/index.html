Dense answers are tiring

A long, packed AI answer can be exhausting before you finish reading it. How much information feels comfortable differs from person to person, and from day to day.

Fit the answer, not the person

Attune builds a small reading-comfort profile and reshapes every answer to match it.

  • Amount

    How much to say: from a one-line summary to the full text.

  • Pace

    How fast it arrives: tap per chunk, slow stream or instant.

  • Shape

    How it is laid out: prose, summary first, bullets or small chunks.

How it works

  1. Step 1

    Measure

    Three short game-like tasks (about 4 minutes) show how you take in information.

  2. Step 2

    Profile

    You get a personal profile: amount, pace and shape. It stays in your browser.

  3. Step 3

    Apply

    The demo reshapes an answer to your profile. Developers can plug in a model through a small adapter.

  4. Step 4

    Adjust (optional)

    On a tired day, a condition signal lightens the answer by a step and suggests a break. Here the signal is virtual; real wearables are on the roadmap.

Questions

Is this a medical test?

No. It is a comfort-preference tool for formatting AI answers. It does not diagnose or treat anything, and the sensor values here are virtual.

Which AI does it work with?

Today it reshapes the built-in sample and any text you paste into the demo. There is no browser extension yet. Developers can connect their own model through the adapter interface.

What happens to my data?

Your profile (scores and levels) is stored in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded and no conversation is stored. Export or delete it any time.

Can I measure again?

Yes, any time. Retake the check-in whenever you like.

Do I need a wearable?

No. The check-in works on its own. The daily-condition step uses virtual values here; connecting real devices is a future, opt-in step and is not available.

Can companies use it?

The license allows noncommercial use only; commercial use needs a separate agreement. There is no API or pricing yet. To ask, open an issue on GitHub or contact the author through the GitHub profile.
